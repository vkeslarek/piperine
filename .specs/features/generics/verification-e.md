## Judged areas

- **Stdlib items against §22, with closed-form oracles.** `source_stdlib_generic.rs` computes every expected value in Rust from the rule it cites (`Section22`, `MonitorSchedule`, `Integrals`, `Laplace`, `Difference`, `TableModel`), never from the model. `since` follows §22.1.8 rules 2 and 3 (the first commit at (0, 0) is a change; T106's reading of the text is right, design-e.md's table was not). `MinPulse`, `Setup`, `Hold`, `Stable` are exact per instant at `u8` and at an enum, with both outcomes present. `inertial`'s oracle (cancel when the next change precedes delivery; emit only a change of the held value) is the §22.5.8 filter. `Short` holds 3 V and 1 mA to 1e-6/1e-9 over two natures. `noise_table` holds 1e-9 relative at a table point, between points (1e4 Hz, linear in f) and past the last (constant), which is `1C`. Holds.
- **`idtmod` across wraps.** `wrapped(at)` is `offset + (rate·t − offset).rem_euclid(modulus)`; over 12 us at 0.3 MV/s the integral wraps four times (at 1.67, 5, 8.33, 11.67 us) and the reads at k + 0.5 us sit away from them; the `SPEC_DEVIATION` guard is gone and S9 left `GAPS.md`. Holds.
- **`laplace_*`/`zi_*` numerics.** Probed beyond the checks: a biproper `laplace_zp::<1, 1>` (zero at −2M), a high-pass `laplace_nd::<2, 2>`, a third-order `laplace_zp::<0, 3>` with a complex pair, a second-order `zi_zp::<0, 2>` with poles 0.5 ± 0.3j, and `zi_nd::<3, 2>`. All match the closed form or the Rust difference equation (laplace worst 3.3e-7 first order, 3.1e-6 third order; zi exact to 1e-6). The implementation is right; the laplace test's tolerance is not (finding 3).
- **The 5e-3 laplace tolerance**: written inline in the test (`source_stdlib_generic.rs:429,432`), not in a contract. No project rule requires a contract for a closed-form oracle (`ToleranceContract` is the ngspice oracles' place), so the location is acceptable; the value is not (finding 3).
- **`inertial` only at a type with `Default`.** Part I §3.3.4 says `bool`, `UInt`, `Int`, `Float` and `str` do not implement `Default` "on purpose", and §22.5.8 bounds `T: Repr + Default` (rule 7). `inertial_at_a_type_without_default_is_refused` pins `E0187` naming `Default`. The AFK default is right and approved. Note (no finding): §22.5.8 rule 4, "It delays a `Bit` or the drive of a resolved net alike", reads as if `Bit<bool>` were allowed; it means the carrier `Bit<T>`. A wording question for `spec-closure`, not this phase.
