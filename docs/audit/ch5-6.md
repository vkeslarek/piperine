## Chapter 5 synthesis (Analog behavior)

The core contribution-statement machinery (5.5-5.6 direct/indirect
branch access, `<+`, the four controlled sources, resistor/RLC idioms) and
the bulk of the procedural surface (5.7-5.9: analog assignments, `if`/`case`,
`repeat`/`while`/`for`, the restricted analog `for`) parse and elaborate as
grammar-conformant (Annex A.6.1/A.6.4/A.6.6-A.6.8/A.6.10 are cited
`Implemented` in `annex_a_grammar.rs` and this was independently spot-checked
against `resolve/pom/expr.rs` and `flatten/analog.rs`). The event-control
subsection (5.10) is where the real divergences concentrate: two of the four
monitored-event functions are broken in different ways (`cross`'s direction
argument silently dropped, `absdelta` entirely absent), the global events
(`initial_step`/`final_step`) and `above`/`timer` are conformant, and named
events (5.10.4) plus digital-event sensitivity from analog code (5.10.5) are
both unimplemented — the latter by an *active, self-documented* Annex C
rejection that inverts what the LRM requires. The Laplace-operator bug (filed
under 5.6's operator surface) is a second confirmed instance of the same
"registered under the wrong name" failure mode as the direction-argument bug:
code that looks complete at a skim (`laplace_*` has a registration loop, a
`LaplaceKind` enum with all four variants, symbolic-diff support) is actually
broken for the two most common LRM spellings because the *registration keys*
don't match the *reserved keywords*.

## Chapter 6 synthesis (Hierarchical structures)

Module declaration, both port-list styles, ordered/named parameter overrides,
and `generate for`/`if`/`case` (6.6, via the separate `UnrollGenerate` pass
per MD-25) are grammar- and elaboration-conformant — confirmed by
`annex_a_grammar.rs`'s A.4.1/A.4.2 `Implemented` entries and by
`elab/lower/generate.rs`'s documented, tested scope. The chapter's
divergences cluster around three features that all share one shape: the
grammar production exists and is faithfully parsed into the AST/POM, but
elaboration never *does* anything with it — `defparam` (6.3.1), `paramset`
(6.4), and array-of-instances (6.2.2, the one FORM-level gap: the array
syntax cannot even be parsed). `defparam` is the most dangerous of the three
because it fails silently with no diagnostic at all, producing a
wrong-but-plausible simulation result rather than a compile error — the
project's stated "fail loud, never silently emit a no-op" principle
(CLAUDE.md, codegen scope) is violated here at the elaborator level.
Connect-module insertion (ch. 7, out of this audit's ch.5-6 scope but
touched by 6.5's port-connection rules) is separately, and already,
self-documented as incomplete in `elab/lower/connect.rs`'s own module doc —
confirmed present but not re-audited here since it's chapter 7 territory.
